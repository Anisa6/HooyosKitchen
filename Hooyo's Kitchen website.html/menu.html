Baaris $7.00

Baaris is a traditional Somali rice dish that is known for its rich flavours and comforting appeal. It typically consists of long-grain rice cooked with a variety of aromatic spices such as cinnamon, cardamom, cloves, and cumin. Baaris often includes tender pieces of meat, such as goat or chicken, which are seasoned and cooked alongside the rice to infuse it with their savory taste. The dish is usually served with a side of flavourful broth or sauce, and it is a staple at Somali gatherings and celebrations. Baaris is loved for its hearty nature and the way it brings people together around the dining table to enjoy a delicious and satisfying meal.
