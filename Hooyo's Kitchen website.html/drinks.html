Mango Shake $5.00

Mango shake is a refreshing and creamy beverage made with ripe mangoes, milk, sugar, vanilla icecream. It's a perfect blend of sweet and tangy flavors, with the natural sweetness of mangoes shining through. The ripe mangoes are peeled, diced, and blended with milk and sugar until smooth and creamy.It is then topped off with rich vanilla bean ice cream.

Shaax $1.75

Shaax is a popular Somali tea that is enjoyed by many. It's a delicious and aromatic beverage made with black tea leaves, spices like cardamom, cloves, ginger, cinnamon, and sometimes a touch of milk and sugar. The tea is brewed with the spices to create a rich and flavourful drink that is often served hot. Whether you prefer it plain or with a hint of sweetness, shaax is a wonderful way to relax and enjoy a cup of warmth and comfort.
