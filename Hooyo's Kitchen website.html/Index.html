About Us

Hooyo's Kitchen is a Somali cuisine restaurant in Toronto, Ontario. We have a diverse menu with traditional Somali dishes and fusion options that blend Somali flavors with other influences. The restaurant has a warm and inviting atmosphere, friendly staff, and authentic flavors. It's a great place to experience the rich culinary heritage of Somalia while enjoying good food and conversation with locals and visitors. The ambiance is welcoming with culturally inspired decor and maybe even traditional music playing in the background. It's a community gathering spot where you can immerse yourself in Somali cuisine and culture.
